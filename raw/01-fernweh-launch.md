# Fernweh Robotics Unveils "Sparrow" Delivery Drone

*TechWire Daily — March 3, 2026*

Berlin-based startup **Fernweh Robotics** today announced **Sparrow**, a lightweight autonomous delivery drone designed for last-mile grocery and pharmacy delivery in dense urban areas. The company says Sparrow can carry payloads of up to 2.5kg over a 12km round trip and lands on rooftop pads to avoid street-level congestion.

Fernweh was founded in 2023 by **Mira Kessler**, a former propulsion engineer, and has raised €14M in seed and Series A funding. The company currently employs 38 people and operates a single test corridor in Berlin's Kreuzberg district.

Kessler said in a statement: "Sparrow is built for cities, not warehouses. We're not trying to compete with long-haul cargo drones — we're solving the last 800 meters."

Fernweh has not yet applied for commercial flight certification, but says it is "in early conversations with regulators" and does not expect any regulatory hurdles before its planned 2027 commercial launch. The company plans to expand testing to Hamburg and Munich later this year.
