# Aerotote Systems Raises €40M Series B to Scale Suburban Drone Delivery

*TechWire Daily — May 6, 2026*

**Aerotote Systems**, a Munich-based drone logistics company, has closed a €40M Series B led by Nordwind Ventures, with participation from several existing investors. The company plans to use the funding to scale its fleet of heavy-lift delivery drones across suburban and exurban regions in Germany and Austria.

Unlike Fernweh Robotics, which focuses on dense urban cores with small, lightweight drones, Aerotote's drones are built to carry payloads up to 9kg over ranges of 25km, targeting lower-density areas where road-based delivery is slower and less efficient. Aerotote sells its drone hardware directly to logistics partners, rather than operating a delivery service itself.

Aerotote CEO Jonas Reiter downplayed direct rivalry with Fernweh: "We don't see much overlap. Dense urban delivery and suburban long-range delivery are genuinely different engineering problems — different payloads, different range requirements, different regulatory postures."

Founded in 2022, Aerotote now employs roughly 70 people and has completed over 3,000 test flights. The company says it has already begun the formal certification process with national aviation authorities, a step it called "table stakes before any commercial rollout."
